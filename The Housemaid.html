John was happy with his recently bought house. A very charming antique one, actually.

He was surprised at how well the house has been kept.

“They really worked hard to keep this house presentable, every time I’m here it’s always shining bright and clean”, he thought.

While moving in all his belongings to the house, surprise struck him while placing his things on the living room cabinet. Apparently there was a hole below it.

“More like a passageway” he said out loud, upon further inspection.

It appeared to connect 2 surrounding rooms, which used to be bedrooms.

“Seems a bit tight, at least for an adult”, John thought but still went to explore.

Sure enough, there was a passage behind a painting in one room, which led down, a bit below the cabinet through the living room, and at the other side there was a ladder leading to a trapdoor in the other room.

John couldn’t bring himself to explore more. He had more work to do to finish moving in. “Maybe next weekend. This could be fun!”

So during his weekday routine, consisting of working during the day and only being home during night to sleep, he began to notice something strange.

“Well, it’s nice that the house is pretty clean, but I’m sure I haven’t been cleaning it these past days”, he realised.

The next day, he decided to investigate a bit further after work.

“Well, maybe I’m imagining things” he said, noticing a bit of dust on the furniture. But the next day, he double checked before going to work and, sure enough, everything was perfectly dusted off.

“OK, this night I’ll figure out what’s going on here.”

So, night came and he went to bed, but determined to stay awake and notice any sound.

Then, he heard a door swing from the kitchen. He contemplated for a bit. “There’s no door at the kitchen, so it must be a secret passage”.

So he got up to check it out.

There was a tall woman with violet hair wearing a maid outfit cleaning the sink.

John decided to be simple and direct.

“Care to explain why you’re here, miss?”

The woman slowly turned to face him, revealing a striking pale skin and an emotionless, apathetic face.

She sounds as emotionless as she seems:

“It is my duty to look after this house, master. Even after my demise.”
